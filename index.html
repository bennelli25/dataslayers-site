ACES Score

Whole-team rankings for youth trap and skeet. Most team scores add up the top few shooters, which rewards two stars and a weak bench. The ACES Score uses the whole spread of a team's 50-target scores.

ACES = Median + Q3 + Q1 + Lower whisker + Max

  • Four of the five parts measure the middle and bottom of the team
  • Each part tops out at 50, so a perfect team scores 250
  • A team of 8 and a team of 80 compare fairly
